# Artificial reef: approval record

- **Piece:** the artificial reef as it looks sunk in the water, one 1x1 building texture, 64x64 RGBA (64 px per cell). Part 2 of ticket 02 (`.scratch/fish-recovery/issues/02-artificial-reef.md`) decides the def, draw layer and deep-water strength.
- **Status:** approved 2026-11-18: candidate C, the steel frame, with visible growth stages.
- **Approved:** 2026-11-18, by the author in chat.
- **Author's words:** "C with visible stages". Asked whether the build should be steel only at a fixed cost, keep its own fixed steel colour (not tinted by stuff) and need Spearfishing and Smithing, the author answered "15 yes".
- **Candidates:**
  - A stone blocks: a cairn of three cut blocks with a fourth laid across them. `ArtificialReef-A-stone-blocks.png`
  - B reef ball: a cast concrete dome with six round holes. `ArtificialReef-B-reef-ball.png`
  - C steel frame: a welded pyramid frame, seen from above as a square with legs and crossbars. `ArtificialReef-C-steel-frame.png`
- **Compared:** `work/contact-sheets/artificial-reef-candidates.png` (ignored), regenerated by `python art/artificial-reef/artificial_reef_art.py art/artificial-reef --sheet work/contact-sheets/artificial-reef-candidates.png`. For each candidate it shows three granite builds on bare floor between the approved reef overlay and the brown kelp overlay, over shallow and deep sea at 32 px per cell. It also shows close-ups tinted steel, granite, sandstone and marble at 64 px per cell, and a proposed growth sequence (built, half way, nearly reef, reef). The sheet is a model, not a capture: flat sea fills sampled from a noon capture of Waves' water, plain alpha blending, and the build at full strength at both depths.
- **Design notes:**
  - Greyscale, so it takes its stuff colour: RimWorld multiplies a stuff-made building's texture by the stuff colour. The sheet uses vanilla `stuffProps` colours from `Data/Core/Defs` (Steel `(105,105,105)`, Granite `(105,95,97)`, Sandstone `(126,104,94)`, Marble `(132,135,132)`), read 2026-11-18. If part 2 gives it a fixed cost instead, the pick needs a fixed colour; C reads as steel only, so it suits steel-only or fixed colour best.
  - No black vanilla outline, deliberately: the art guide's 2 px outline is for objects above the water, and the approved reef overlay, which this sits beside under the surface, has none. A soft dark rim and a faint contact shadow to the lower right (light from the upper left) stand in for it. Body alpha is 0.8 (the reef overlay's is about 0.55) so it reads as built but the sea shows through.
  - The growth stages darken the top surfaces to the reef overlay's colour and fade toward its alpha. They are a proposal; part 2 decides whether the building changes look as it grows.
- **Source:** procedural, drawn by `artificial_reef_art.py` with fixed seeds; `python artificial_reef_art.py <out_dir>` regenerates every candidate and its 256 px master byte for byte. No reference images, and no Waves, Living Waters or vanilla textures were used.
- **Export recipe:** each 256 px master (`*_master256.png`, 4x) reduced to 64 px with Lanczos.
- **Exported textures:** `Textures/Things/Spearfishing/Building/ArtificialReef/`, written by `python art/artificial-reef/artificial_reef_art.py art/artificial-reef --textures Textures`:
  - `ArtificialReef.png`: built, the C candidate unchanged (byte for byte `ArtificialReef-C-steel-frame.png`).
  - `ArtificialReef_HalfWay.png`: `crust()` at 0.5, the sheet's "half way".
  - `ArtificialReef_NearlyReef.png`: `crust()` at 0.9, the sheet's "nearly reef".
  - The fourth stage on the sheet, "reef", is no texture: the building removes itself and the cell shows the reef overlay.
- **Colour:** the textures stay greyscale as approved. The game draws them multiplied by vanilla Steel's colour `(105,105,105)`, the def's fixed `graphicData` colour, which is the sheet's steel close-up; the build has no stuff, so nothing else tints it. The growth crust is tinted with it, as the sheet's stages were with granite.
- **In game (ticket 02 part 2):** drawn under the water at Waves' sea-life queue like the reef overlay, at full strength over shallow ocean and 0.7 over deep, matching the floor overlay. The stage shown is built for the first third of the 15 days, half way for the second, nearly reef for the last.

Approval is final: a change to these pixels, the size or the colour is a new candidate that needs a new approval.
