# Kelp overlay: approval record

- **Pieces:** two looks of the one kelp floor, each a 512x512 RGBA texture that tiles every 8x8 cells at 64 px per cell, drawn like the reef overlay (translucent over kelp cells at Waves' sea-life render queue, edges faded, deep kelp at 0.7 strength).
  - Brown, for maps under 15°C: `Textures/Spearfishing/Terrain/KelpOverlayBrown.png`, candidate B2.
  - Olive, for maps from 15 to 22°C: `Textures/Spearfishing/Terrain/KelpOverlayOlive.png`, candidate B.

- **Author's words:** first pick "B but make them darker brown lookup up kelp in cape town for reference"; then "B2 but keep B maybe use it in slightly warmer waters"; then, for the rule, "0-15 brown 15-22 b, 22+ reefs also". The orchestrator recorded B2 as approved as it is, regular fans included, and B's olive fronds as they appear on the first sheet.

Both sheets show each overlay tiled at 32 px per cell over flat shallow and deep sea colours sampled from an in-game capture, with the patch-edge fade.
