# No DLC needed; with Odyssey, spearfishing shares Odyssey's fish

Amended by ADR 0013: with Odyssey, its population also limits dives, so the fish are shared both ways.

Spearfishing works without any DLC, like Waves, so it keeps its own local fish stock rather than using Odyssey's fish population, which treats a whole ocean as one water body and so cannot make a reef better than open sand. When Odyssey is active, a catch is Odyssey's own fish item and also lowers Odyssey's population for that water, so line fishing and spearfishing draw on the same fish. Without Odyssey, every catch is one generic raw fish item of our own.

## Consequences

Spearfishing has its own work type, present with or without Odyssey, so work priorities in a save don't change when the DLC is toggled. Its skill comes from the spear (Melee or Shooting), not Odyssey's Animals.

## Considered Options

- Require Odyssey and use its population as the stock: least content, but no local reef or kelp effect.
- Ignore Odyssey: duplicate fish items for Odyssey players and two unconnected fish counts.
