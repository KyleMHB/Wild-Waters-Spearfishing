# Higher gear tiers use vanilla research, not our own projects

The industrial and spacer gear needs no research project of Spearfishing's own. Each item needs the mod's medieval Spearfishing research and the vanilla research that fits it: Machining for the band speargun and low-volume mask, Biofuel refining for the chemfuel-made plastic and rubber gear (plastic fins, neoprene wetsuit, dry snorkel, foam buoy), Advanced fabrication for the pneumatic speargun, long carbon fins and rebreather, and Recon armor for the thermal wetsuit. The author chose this on 2026-11-16 to prevent research bloat, and so each item sits in the era its technology belongs to.

## Considered Options

- "Diving gear" (industrial) and "Advanced diving gear" (spacer) projects of our own, each after a vanilla project: groups the gear in one place on the tree, but adds two projects that only gate items, and the spec had planned them until this decision.

## Consequences

- Gear unlocks are spread over several vanilla projects, so the research tab has no one place that lists them; item descriptions and the Workshop page must say what unlocks each.
- A colony can unlock an item without meaning to, by researching Machining for other reasons; it still needs Spearfishing first.
- An item's bench can add a gate. The dry snorkel is made at the machining table (the author's choice on 2026-11-17, over a smithy), so it needs Machining as well as Biofuel refining.
- The spacer head piece changed on 2026-11-17 from a full-face mask to a rebreather in the snorkel's slot. Its research stays Spearfishing and Advanced fabrication.
