Your colonists swim out with spears, bring fish home from the sea, lakes and rivers, and snorkel near shore to relax.

RimWorld: Waves fills the water with fish you can watch but never catch. Spearfishing lets you paint a zone on the sea, a deep lake or a deep river and send divers to fish it. Each body of water has one fish stock that all its zones share, so how hard you fish matters.

[b]Mod version:[/b] 0.1.0
[b]Game versions:[/b] 1.6 and 1.7

[h2]Getting started[/h2]
[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/KyleMHB/Wild-Waters-Spearfishing/main/docs/images/workshop/spearfishing-getting-started.png[/img]
[olist]
[*]Paint a spearfishing zone from the Zone menu on deep water, over reef or kelp where you can.
[*]Give a colonist a spear or speargun and enable Spearfishing in the Work tab.
[*]Allow raw fish in a stockpile. Divers leave the catch on the shore.
[/olist]

[h2]Diving[/h2]
[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/KyleMHB/Wild-Waters-Spearfishing/main/docs/images/workshop/spearfishing-dive.gif[/img]
Spearfishing is its own work type. Spear dives use Melee and speargun dives use Shooting. A diver wades out, slips under the surface and tries for a fish every few seconds. They head home when the stringer is full, the water is fished down, or rest or warmth runs low.

[h2]Fish stock[/h2]
[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/KyleMHB/Wild-Waters-Spearfishing/main/docs/images/workshop/spearfishing-shoal.gif[/img]
[list]
[*]Water holds one fish per cell, up to 2400. A catch anywhere on that water lowers it, and an ordinary sea regrows about 60 fish a day.
[*]Shoals swim the water and scatter from divers. Fewer fish means fewer shoals.
[*]Each zone has a fish-down limit, 50% by default, from 10% to 90%. Its divers stop below it.
[/list]

[h2]Reef and kelp[/h2]
[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/KyleMHB/Wild-Waters-Spearfishing/main/docs/images/workshop/spearfishing-kelp.gif[/img]
New coastal maps get reef, and coasts under 22°C get kelp that sways in the swell. Fish gather over both, so dives there land half again as often, and they speed regrowth up to double for water that is all reef.

[h2]Fish recovery[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Artificial reef.[/b] A steel frame on bare sea floor, 40 steel. After 15 days its cell becomes reef.
[*][b]Habitat restoration.[/b] Mark bare sea floor with [b]Restore sea floor[/b]. Colonists on Spearfishing work replant cuttings from nearby reef or kelp using Plants, and restored floor spreads slowly within the marks.
[*][b]Hatchery.[/b] A powered building fed hay, kibble or raw plant food. It fills a pail of 30 fry every 3 days, and a hauler releases them into the water you choose.
[/list]

[h2]Threats[/h2]
The storyteller sends these about once a year per map, and each kind has its own switch. They slow regrowth but never take fish.
[list]
[*][b]Urchins[/b] graze a patch of sea reef and kelp bare, and the barren grows every 3 days. Divers cull it with Melee, and culled cells regrow in 5 days.
[*][b]Invasive predators[/b], lionfish at sea and snakeheads in fresh water, cut a water's regrowth by up to half. Spearfishing there culls them.
[/list]

[h2]Gear[/h2]
Spears and spearguns are real weapons. Fins add swim speed, masks dive success, wetsuits let divers work colder water, and buoys hold more fish and keep an exhausted diver afloat.

[h2]Sharks and snorkeling[/h2]
[list]
[*]Over deep ocean, a shark may take a diver's catch, 1% a trip by default. A diver with a speargun may drive it off. It is a shadow, never an animal.
[*]In their recreation time, colonists snorkel near shore with no gear, zone or research, so pacifists can too.
[/list]

[h2]Settings[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Dive visuals[/b], [b]Fish stock management[/b], [b]Urchin events[/b] and [b]Invasive predator events[/b] are on by default.
[*]With management off, water regrows a flat amount each day, divers stop at a quarter, and recovery and threats are gone. Turning it off removes recovery buildings and marks and refunds them. Floor that became reef stays.
[*][b]Shark chance per trip[/b] runs from 0 to 20%.
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility and saves[/h2]
[list]
[*]Load after RimWorld: Waves.
[*]You can add it to an existing save, or remove it. Removing it drops the mod's zones, floor, buildings, gear and fish, with one-time errors about missing defs on the first load.
[/list]
